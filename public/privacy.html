Privacy Policy

KROFT by Orynthra · Last updated 1 November 2026

KROFT is a personal assistant for your money, schedule, contacts, notes and wellbeing. To do that it has to hold personal information, so this page explains plainly what we collect, why, who else handles it, and the control you have over it. "We", "us" and "Orynthra" mean the company that makes KROFT.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, and the email address or phone number you sign in with. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • What you put into KROFT: income and expenses, budgets, tasks, reminders, notes, appointments, contacts, projects and documents, files and photos you attach, voice memos, chat messages, and wellness entries such as mood, sleep and journal notes.
  • Linked accounts: if you connect Google or Microsoft, we store the access tokens those services give us so KROFT can read and send email and read and create calendar events for you. We only use them for the features you turn on.
  • Location: only when you use Around Me, and only after your device asks for permission. Your location is used for that search and is not stored.
  • Subscription status: if you subscribe to KROFT Plus, we keep your plan status and renewal dates. Card details are handled by our payment provider and never reach us.
  • Usage counts: how many times you have used metered features (such as voice) today or this month, so free-plan limits can be applied.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, your browser's push address and the times of the reminders it should deliver.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics, and we do not sell your information.

How we use it

  • To run KROFT for you: storing your data, syncing it between your devices, and answering your requests.
  • To generate AI replies, summaries, suggestions and reports from your own data when you ask, or when you have turned on automations.
  • To send the reminders and notifications you set up.
  • To process your subscription and apply plan limits.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems you report to us.

AI and your data

When you chat with KROFT, or it prepares a brief, suggestion or report, the relevant parts of your data are sent with your message to our AI provider (Google's Gemini API) to produce the reply. Wellness data is only included if Wellness Data in KROFT Chat is on in Privacy center. KROFT's replies are generated by AI, can be wrong, and are not financial, medical, legal or tax advice.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of service providers, each only for the job described:

  • Supabase: sign-in and database storage for your account.
  • Vercel: hosting for the app and its server functions.
  • Google (Gemini API): generating AI replies.
  • Flutterwave: processing KROFT Plus payments.
  • Google and Microsoft: your own mail and calendar, only if you link them.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass): finding nearby places for Around Me. Only the search and an approximate location are sent.
  • Your browser and device: speech recognition, read-aloud voices, push notifications and Face ID are provided by your browser or operating system, under its own privacy terms.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

Where it is stored and how long we keep it

With an account, your data is stored with our providers and may be processed outside your country. It is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our database straight away; copies in our providers' backups expire on their normal schedule. Without an account, KROFT keeps your data only on your device.

Your choices and rights

  • See and copy your data: Profile → History and data → Download backup.
  • Correct it: edit anything directly in the app.
  • Disconnect linked accounts: Profile → Connected apps.
  • Limit AI access to wellness data: Profile → Privacy center.
  • Delete everything: Profile → Privacy center → Delete account.

Depending on where you live, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and the GDPR, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us to use any of these rights.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each account's data is separated at the database level so no one else can read it, and linked-account tokens are only ever accessible to our servers. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us if you notice anything wrong.

Children

KROFT is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact
